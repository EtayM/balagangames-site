Reporting, blocking, and removal

  • Report: any player can report another player from the game's PLAYERS menu, in any room. The reported player is not told who reported them. A real person reviews every report, and we aim to act within 24 hours.
  • Block: in a public room, a player can block another player. They leave the room and won't be matched with that player again.
  • Room hosts and votes: the host of a private room can remove anyone from it. In a public room, the other players can vote a player out. Either way, that player can't rejoin that room.
  • Bans: if a report shows these rules were broken, we can ban the player from public rooms, without notice. A ban is either temporary (for example a day, a week or a month), and ends by itself when its time is up, or permanent. The game tells a banned player when their ban ends.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026 · Applies to: Sneaklings — Paint Hide & Seek and other Balagan Games titles

The short version: Sneaklings has no user accounts, no ads, and no analytics. We do not run our own servers and do not sell your personal data. The only information we look at is what a player report contains, and we use it only to keep the game safe. Online play and purchases are handled by the platform services described below. Playing online also means agreeing to our Terms of Use.

Who we are

Balagan Games is the studio name of Etay Matzliah, an independent game developer registered in Israel. You can reach us at support@balagangames.com.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not require or offer user accounts, registration, or sign-in.
  • We do not show advertisements.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties for marketing.
  • We do not operate servers that store your data.

Online multiplayer (Epic Online Services)

Sneaklings uses Epic Online Services (EOS), operated by Epic Games, Inc., for lobbies, matchmaking, and connecting players. To make this work:

  • The game creates an anonymous device identifier on your device. It is not linked to your name, email, or any account, and is used only to keep you connected to game sessions.
  • Your IP address is processed transiently by Epic's infrastructure to route game traffic between players. We never see or store it.
  • The game also creates a random player ID on your device. It is shared with the other players' devices in your room so that you can rejoin after a dropped connection and so that a removed player can't come back in. It is not linked to your name, email, or any account.
  • The nickname you enter and your in-game actions (such as paint strokes) are visible to the other players in your session while you play. They are not stored after the session ends, except inside a player report (below).

Epic Games processes this data under its own privacy policy: Epic Games Privacy Policy.

Player reports, blocking, and bans

To keep rooms safe, players can report each other from inside the game (see our Terms of Use). When you send a report, the game sends it to Epic's player reports service, part of Epic Online Services, where we review it. A report contains:

  • the reason you picked;
  • the anonymous Epic identifiers of you and of the reported player for that session;
  • the reported player's nickname and ban code (a one-way scrambled form of their random player ID; it can't be turned back into the ID);
  • the game mode, room code, and round;
  • possibly a small picture of the reported player's in-game paint, so we can see what was reported.

The reported player is never told who reported them. We use reports only to moderate the game and never share them with anyone else. Reports stay in Epic's service, under Epic's privacy policy; we keep no copies of our own.

If a player is banned, their ban code is added to a list published on this website, which the game checks before joining public rooms. The list contains ban codes only: no names and no other information.

If you block a player, the game remembers their ban code on your device only, so you aren't matched with them again. When you join a public room, your block list is shared with that room's host so the game can keep you apart. The game also remembers on your device that you agreed to the Terms of Use.

Purchases

In-app purchases and paid downloads are processed entirely by the platform store you use — Google Play (Google), the App Store (Apple), or Steam (Valve). We never receive your payment details. Refunds and payment questions are handled by the respective store under its own policies.

Children

Sneaklings is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Online sessions can include other players, so we recommend that younger teens play with people they know.

Data security and retention

We do not store personal data on servers of our own. Player reports are kept in Epic's service only as long as we need them to moderate. Ban codes stay on the ban list for as long as the ban lasts. Data processed by Epic Games or the platform stores is governed by their policies, linked above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or similar laws), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. For reports or bans that concern you, email us. Requests concerning other multiplayer or purchase data should be directed to Epic Games or your platform store. If you are unsure, email us and we will point you the right way.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the game handles data (for example, adding a new online feature), we will update this page and change the effective date above.

Paint yourself into the scene and hope nobody notices.

Online hide-and-seek party game for 2–8 players. Free on phones, with a room code your friends join in seconds.

Watch a round

Hiding, painting, and getting caught.

How it plays

Hiders disguise themselves as things in the scene. Seekers tap anything that looks a little too alive.

1Pick a disguise

A jar, a lantern, a rubber duck: choose a shape from the shelf and wear it.

2Paint yourself in

Copy a prop, melt into the wall, or paint something so dumb nobody looks twice.

3Hold still

Seekers hunt with a flashlight. Survive, then see everyone's disguise in the gallery.

Friends join in seconds

Make a room, read out the code or show the QR. Your friends play free on their phones.

Sneaklings press kit

Paint yourself into the scene and hope nobody notices. Sneaklings is online hide-and-seek for 2–8 players: hiders turn into garden gnomes, cheese wheels and toy dinosaurs and paint themselves to match the map, while seekers hunt for anything that looks a little too alive.

Features

  • Hide by painting. Brushes, spray, fill and an eyedropper that pulls colours from the map.
  • A new crate of disguises every round, drawn from 98.
  • Seekers get clues. A plate at the top of the screen narrows down the disguises as the clock runs out.
  • Water balloons. Hiders can taunt the seekers for bonus points, at the price of a hint.
  • Every disguise revealed at the end of each round, from the masterpieces to the disasters.
  • Play with anyone: a 4-letter code or QR, friends on PC and phones in one room. In a private room, the host's maps are everyone's maps.
  • Share a hiding spot as a short clip from the gallery. PC saves a picture.

About the studio

Balagan Games makes party games you play with friends. Sneaklings is our first game, made by one developer.

AI disclosure

The map illustrations, the disguise silhouettes, parts of the emote art, some interface art, the title lettering, the music, the emote voices and some sound effects were made with generative AI tools, and the code was written with AI coding agents. Nothing is generated while you play: every player's paint is their own, and the mascot is painted, not generated.
